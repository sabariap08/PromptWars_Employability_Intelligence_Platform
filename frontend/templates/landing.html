How the platform works

Every stage produces evidence that feeds the next. Nothing is a dead end — assessments, projects and trainer feedback all change readiness.

1

Join an approved institution

Learners register under a university or company. Admins approve memberships after email verification.

2

Build an evidence profile

Skills, certifications, projects and a target role create the context every agent uses.

3

Take a baseline assessment

Deterministic scoring plus agent analysis produces explainable topic-level gaps.

4

Follow an adaptive roadmap

Tasks adapt as new evidence arrives. Industry experts verify practical checkpoints.

One platform, five roles

Explainable by design

Scoring is deterministic and auditable. AI providers add narrative and recommendations, with a deterministic fallback when no key is configured.

Readiness you can defend

Required and preferred skills are weighted, then adjusted for verification strength: self-reported, partial, verified by assessment, or verified by a trainer.
